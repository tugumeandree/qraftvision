About Qraft Academy

Qraft Academy, a program by Rata Qraft Solutions, empowers entrepreneurs, freelancers, professionals, and investors with lifelong learning and development. We focus on developing mindset, skillset, and toolset for success in the modern world of work.

Through Qraft Labs and Qraft Solutions Hub, we deliver content development and training, software development, talent development, funding, and modern workspaces for our community.

Programs at Qraft Academy

1. WorkMasters

A work readiness program with Bootcamps, 3-month Internships, and 6-month Apprenticeships across three tracks: Content Creators, Technologists, and Growth Professionals.

WorkMasters Bootcamps run weekly, open to the public for UGX 25,000 per class or UGX 500,000 for 6 months. Apprentices and interns attend for free and receive stipends.

2. Future of Work Consortium

A 5-year movement to equip 100M workers in 8 emerging economies through tours, training, and strategic collaborations. Built on 12 Future of Work Principles and 5 Strategic Pillars: Academy, Accelerator, Research & Reports, R&D, and Workforce Capital.

Qraft Solutions Hub

A network and support center for remote workers, gig workers, and startups with access to modern workspaces, investment programs, and referral opportunities.

Oversees prizes and awards for the Future of Work Consortium and supports members in branding, placements, and global opportunities.

Offers net worth development through investments and net weight growth by profiling, branding, and linking members to business process outsourcing, gigs, placements, and other opportunities.

Qraft Labs

Qraft Labs is the research and development engine of Qraft Academy that supports innovation across 8 economic frontiers—care, experience, gig, trade, green/circular, digital, creative, and knowledge economies.

It provides a platform for researchers, innovators, and future-focused entrepreneurs to explore ideas, prototype solutions, and collaborate with peers and experts. Qraft Labs promotes experimentation, co-creation, and problem-solving in real-world workforce challenges.

Participants benefit from mentorship, collaborative spaces, research resources, and integration into larger innovation programs like the Future of Work Consortium and Qraft Solutions Hub.
