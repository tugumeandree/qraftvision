Draft Curricula for CapitalMasters Training Programs

Here are draft curricula for the training programs across the different financial system levels in the CapitalMasters program.

Informal Financial Sector (VSLAs, SHGs, Money Lenders)

1. Group Formation and Leadership Training

  • Principles of cooperative and group formation
  • Leadership roles and responsibilities
  • Democratic decision-making processes
  • Building trust and teamwork

2. Basic Savings and Loan Management

  • Importance of savings and record-keeping
  • Collecting, safekeeping, and managing group funds
  • Understanding loans, interest, and repayment schedules
  • Basic budgeting skills

3. Bookkeeping and Record-Keeping

  • Using simple cashbooks and ledgers
  • Maintaining member passbooks
  • Tracking loans and repayments
  • Preparing basic financial reports

4. Conflict Resolution and Governance

  • Identifying and managing conflicts
  • Communication and negotiation skills
  • Establishing group rules and codes of conduct
  • Roles of committees and accountability mechanisms

5. Financial Literacy and Digital Literacy

  • Understanding financial products and services available
  • Basics of budgeting for members
  • Introduction to mobile money and digital transactions
  • Fraud awareness and consumer protection

Microfinance and SACCO Development (Tier IV)

1. Advanced Governance and Management

  • Strategic planning and organizational structures
  • Roles of the Board and Management Committee
  • Internal controls and audit processes
  • Reporting and accountability

2. Product Innovation and Risk Management

  • Designing savings and loan products
  • Risk identification, mitigation, and monitoring
  • Loan appraisal and portfolio diversification
  • Pricing and interest rate setting

3. Digital Financial Services Adoption

  • Introduction to digital platforms and mobile banking for SACCOs
  • Client onboarding and digital transaction management
  • Data security and privacy best practices

4. Compliance with UMRA Regulations

  • Overview of UMRA licensing requirements
  • Reporting and regulatory submissions
  • Anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing requirements

5. Linkage Building with Formal Financial Institutions

  • Building partnerships with banks and MFIs
  • Referral systems and credit guarantee schemes
  • Accessing grants and funding opportunities

Microfinance Deposit-Taking Institutions (Tier III)

1. Regulatory Compliance and Reporting

  • Bank of Uganda regulations for MDIs
  • Financial reporting standards and timelines
  • Risk management frameworks

2. Credit Risk Assessment and Portfolio Management

  • Credit appraisal techniques
  • Monitoring and managing loan delinquency
  • Debt recovery and restructuring practices

3. Customer Relationship Management

  • Customer service excellence
  • Client retention and loyalty programs
  • Feedback and grievance handling mechanisms

4. Technology Integration and Mobile Banking

  • Digital transformation strategies
  • Use of management information systems (MIS)
  • Cybersecurity awareness

5. Financial Inclusion Strategies for Underserved Communities

  • Identifying and designing products for women, youth, rural clients
  • Inclusive finance principles
  • Partnerships for outreach initiatives

Credit Institutions (Tier II)

1. Corporate Governance and Leadership

  • Board roles and fiduciary duties
  • Leadership competencies in financial institutions
  • Ethics and integrity in banking

2. Advanced Credit Analysis and Lending Techniques

  • Financial statement analysis
  • Loan structuring and covenants
  • Sector-specific lending (agriculture, SMEs)

3. Anti-Money Laundering and Fraud Prevention

  • AML/CFT legal framework
  • Fraud risk assessment and internal controls
  • Reporting suspicious transactions

4. Foreign Exchange and Treasury Management

  • Forex market operations
  • Treasury risk management
  • Liquidity management techniques

5. SME and Agricultural Financing Solutions

  • Designing financing products for SMEs and farmers
  • Risk mitigation instruments
  • Value chain finance concepts

Commercial Banks (Tier I)

1. Leadership Development for Financial Institutions

  • Transformational leadership skills
  • Change management
  • Strategic risk management

2. Digital Transformation and Fintech Integration

  • Digital banking trends and innovations
  • Partnering with fintech firms
  • Regulatory technology (RegTech)

3. Sustainable and Inclusive Banking Practices

  • Environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria
  • Designing inclusive financial products
  • Corporate social responsibility programs

4. Capital Markets and Investment Products

  • Overview of capital markets
  • Product development (bonds, equities, derivatives)
  • Investor relations and communication

5. Corporate Social Responsibility and Consumer Protection

  • Consumer rights and ethical banking
  • Complaint handling and dispute resolution
  • Financial education campaigns

Investment Funds and Capital Markets (Highest Level)

1. Investment Analysis and Portfolio Management

  • Fundamental and technical analysis
  • Asset allocation and diversification
  • Performance measurement and evaluation

2. Capital Market Operations and Compliance

  • Securities trading procedures
  • Legal and regulatory environment
  • Market surveillance and investor protection

3. Private Equity and Venture Capital Management

  • Fund structuring and fundraising
  • Due diligence and valuation
  • Exit strategies and returns management

4. Financial Product Innovation and Marketing

  • Designing innovative investment products
  • Marketing strategies and client acquisition
  • Investor education and engagement

5. Investor Education and Protection

  • Understanding investment risks and returns
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Rights and responsibilities of investors
