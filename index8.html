About Qraft Academy

Qraft Academy, a program by Rata Qraft Solutions, empowers entrepreneurs, freelancers, professionals, and investors with lifelong learning and development. We focus on developing mindset, skillset, and toolset for success in the modern world of work.

Through Qraft Labs and Qraft Solutions Hub, we deliver content development and training, software development, talent development, funding, and modern workspaces for our community.

Programs at Qraft Academy

1. WorkMasters

WorkMasters is a comprehensive work readiness program designed to equip participants with essential skills through various learning formats.

Key Components:

  • Masterminds: Collaborative sessions focusing on personal and professional development.
  • Skill-Building Bootcamps: Physical and online training in digital, design, and data skills.
  • Apprenticeships: 6-month programs in specialized tracks with mentorship.

Join the WorkMasters Program to enhance your skills and prepare for the workforce!

2. Future of Work Consortium

A 5-year movement to equip 100M workers in emerging economies through tours, training, and collaborations.

3. 21st Century Investors Program

This program provides opportunities to explore and invest in high-potential assets and innovative ventures.
