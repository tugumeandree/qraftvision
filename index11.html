CourseMasters (Course Development)

About Qraft Academy

Qraft Academy, a program by Rata Qraft Solutions, empowers entrepreneurs, freelancers, professionals, and investors with lifelong learning and development. We focus on developing mindset, skillset, and toolset for success in the modern world of work.

Through Qraft Labs and Qraft Solutions Hub, we deliver content development and training, software development, talent development, funding, and modern workspaces for our community.

Our Pillars

Learning
Skills Development (Data, Digital, and Design), Leadership & Business, Personal & Professional Growth
Working
Qraft Labs for innovation in 8 economies: care, experience, gig, trade, green/circular, digital, creative, and knowledge
Achieving
Through the Qraft Solutions Hub, members grow their network, net worth, and net weight via funding, branding, and outsourcing opportunities
